Status: targeted cadence experiment triggered on the isolated lab branch.

The four-cut historical Wikipedia union is proven to be below the official LPF
players-used lower bound for Banfield, Newell's Old Boys, Godoy Cruz, and
Barracas Central. This bounded follow-up adds six intermediate historical cuts
for only those four clubs: 2024-05-31, 2024-06-30, 2024-08-15, 2024-09-30,
2024-10-31, and 2024-11-30.

The result will be unioned with the already-verified opening, midyear,
window-close, and final snapshots to create a ten-cut deterministic provider-local
membership union per club. Reaching the official used-player count is only a
necessary lower-bound check, never proof that the Wikipedia roster is complete.

No database writes, canonical player creation, or product promotion are part of
this experiment.
