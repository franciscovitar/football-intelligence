# Goalserve pre-trial rights and historical coverage request

Status: draft only — not sent.

## Draft message

Subject: Historical soccer player stats, trial access and post-trial retention questions

Hello Goalserve team,

I am evaluating your Soccer API for a football analytics product. Before starting the free trial, I would like to confirm the exact historical coverage and licence/retention conditions.

Our target competitions are:

- Argentina Primera División / Liga Profesional
- English Premier League
- Spanish La Liga / Primera Division
- Italian Serie A
- German Bundesliga
- French Ligue 1

We are primarily interested in historical player statistics from the 2016 season onward.

Could you please confirm:

1. Coverage
   - For each of the six competitions above, from which season are detailed player statistics broadly available?
   - Are those historical seasons league-wide, or are some clubs/matches only partially covered?
   - Do historical player profiles/season rows include appearances, minutes, starts, goals, assists, shots, passes/key passes, tackles, interceptions, duels, dribbles, cards and average rating?

2. Free trial
   - Does the advertised 30-day free trial include full access to historical player statistics/profile endpoints back to 2016?
   - Is a credit card or other billing method required to activate the trial?
   - Does the trial automatically convert to a paid subscription unless cancelled?

3. Storage and retention
   - May API responses be stored/cache-normalized in our own database during a trial or paid subscription?
   - If we acquire historical data during the free trial and then do not subscribe, may those already acquired historical records remain stored and displayed in our product?
   - If we subscribe for one month and later cancel, may previously acquired historical records remain stored and displayed?
   - May derived analytics calculated from Goalserve data remain stored after access ends?

4. Product/display rights
   - May Goalserve historical soccer data be displayed in a public commercial analytics website if raw feeds are not resold as a standalone feed?
   - Are there any attribution/logo requirements?
   - Are there competition-specific restrictions for the six leagues above?

5. Package and backfill
   - Which minimum soccer package provides the historical player-stat/profile feeds from 2016 onward?
   - Is there a bulk historical export or snapshot option, or must history be acquired endpoint-by-endpoint?

We are not looking to resell your raw feed. The goal is to combine source-scoped Goalserve evidence with other sources inside our own analytics product while preserving provenance.

Thank you.
